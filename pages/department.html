Department Guide

Department & Referral Questions

Common questions about specialist referrals, home medical devices, and virtual physical examinations.

Do I need a GP referral letter to book a specialist department?

No GP referral is required for self-pay patients—you can book directly into Tele-Cardiology, Tele-Neurology, Teledermatology, or any other specialty. If you are using private medical insurance, our 24/7 GP triage desk can issue an instant digital referral letter in 10 minutes.

How do I receive the home ECG, spirometer, or dermatoscope kit?

Once your department appointment or RPM plan is booked, we dispatch a pre-calibrated medical device kit via tracked next-day courier (or 2-hour bike courier within Greater London) with a prepaid return pouch.

What happens if my virtual specialist decides I need an in-person scan or procedure?

Your consultant has direct booking privileges at 140+ partner imaging centers and private hospitals across London, Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh, and Cardiff for priority MRI, CT, ultrasound, or day-case procedures.
