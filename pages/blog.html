Quick Prep Guides

3-Minute Pre-Consultation Checklists

Checklist #1

Before a Cardiac Video Call

  • Rest quietly for 5 mins before BP reading
  • Sync 7-day Apple Watch or ECG patch log
  • Have current medication boxes beside you
  • Note exact time of any palpitations
Checklist #2

Before a Dermatology Upload

  • Use natural indirect daylight near a window
  • Take 1 overview shot + 2 macro close-ups
  • Place a small ruler next to any mole/lesion
  • Avoid applying makeup or tinted creams
Checklist #3

Before a Pediatric Night Call

  • Record child's latest temperature & weight
  • Note time & dose of last paracetamol/ibuprofen
  • Attach clean tip to home digital ear otoscope
  • Have your preferred 24-hr pharmacy postcode ready
