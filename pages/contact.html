Support FAQ

Frequently Asked Support Questions

What should I do if I am experiencing a life-threatening medical emergency?

If you or someone else is experiencing severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden weakness on one side of the body, or severe bleeding, dial 999 immediately in the UK. For urgent non-life-threatening medical triage, call our 24/7 hotline at +91 9876543210.

How can I reschedule or cancel my virtual consultation?

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 30 minutes before your scheduled video slot via the link in your confirmation email, or by calling +91 9876543210.

How do I request a copy of my medical records or consultation invoice?

All clinical notes, lab results, and itemized insurance invoices are available for instant PDF download in your patient portal, or email info@stacklytelemed.co.uk for certified copies.
