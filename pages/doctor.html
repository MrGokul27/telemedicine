Doctor Booking FAQ

Questions About Seeing Our Doctors

Can I book follow-up appointments with the exact same doctor?

Yes. Continuity of care is central to Stackly Telemedicine. You can select your preferred named GP or Consultant for all future video check-ins and message them directly for 7 days after every visit.

Can Stackly Telemedicine doctors write NHS GP letters and private referrals?

Yes. Every clinician can issue formal specialist referral letters, diagnostic imaging requests (MRI, CT, Ultrasound), fit-to-fly certificates, and occupational sick notes signed with their GMC registration number.

How do I verify a doctor's GMC registration number?

Every doctor card on our platform displays their 7-digit General Medical Council (GMC) reference number, which can be verified instantaneously on the official UK GMC online medical register.
