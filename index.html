Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I see a UK doctor on video?

On-demand GP consultations connect in an average of 8 minutes, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Specialist consultant appointments (Cardiology, Neurology, Dermatology, Pediatrics) are available same-day or next-day.

How do digital prescriptions work after my video call?

Immediately after your consultation, your clinician issues a secure cryptographic prescription token via SMS and email. You can present it at any of 980+ UK pharmacies for instant collection or choose same-day courier delivery.

Are my video consultations and medical records private?

Yes. All video streams use end-to-end WebRTC AES-256 encryption and are never recorded without explicit consent. Our infrastructure is ISO 27001 certified, UK GDPR compliant, and NHS Digital DSP Toolkit accredited.

Can I sync my Apple Watch or home blood pressure monitor?

Absolutely. Our patient portal integrates with Apple Health, Google Health Connect, Withings, Omron, and clinical Bluetooth ECG/spirometry devices so your doctor sees live vitals during your appointment.
